🌾 KrishiMitra

Empowering Farmers Through Data-Driven Decisions

An intelligent crop recommendation platform designed to assist farmers in selecting the most suitable crops using Machine Learning, soil analysis and environmental conditions.

About The Project

KrishiMitra is a smart agricultural decision-support system developed to help farmers make informed crop selection decisions.

The system analyzes important parameters such as Nitrogen (N), Phosphorous (P), Potassium (K), Temperature, Humidity, pH Value and Rainfall to estimate the suitability of different crops.

Unlike traditional recommendation systems, KrishiMitra also considers farmer preferences by balancing:

  • Crop Suitability
  • Expected Profitability
  • Expected Yield Per Hectare

This allows recommendations to be more personalized and practical for real-world farming scenarios.
