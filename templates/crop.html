Before You Begin

To get accurate crop recommendations, KrishiMitra needs information about your soil and local weather conditions. These values help the system understand which crops are most suitable and profitable for your farm.

  • N, P, K Values (Nitrogen, Phosphorus, Potassium)

    These are the three major nutrients required for plant growth.

    • Nitrogen (N) helps leaf and stem growth.
    • Phosphorus (P) helps root development and flowering.
    • Potassium (K) improves crop strength and disease resistance.

    Ways to measure:
    • Government Soil Testing Laboratories
    • Soil Health Card Scheme
    • Portable NPK Sensors/Meters
    • Private agricultural labs

    Recommended Method:
    Government soil testing centers and Soil Health Cards are the most reliable, low-cost, and accessible options for Indian farmers.

  • pH Value

    pH tells whether the soil is acidic, neutral, or alkaline. Soil pH strongly affects nutrient absorption and crop growth.

    Ways to measure:
    • Digital pH Meter
    • Soil Testing Kit
    • Government Soil Testing Centers

    Recommended Method:
    A simple soil testing kit or government soil testing center is cheap, accurate enough, and easily available for most farmers.

  • Humidity & Temperature

    These values describe weather conditions around the farm. They affect crop growth, disease spread, and water requirements.

    Ways to measure:
    • Mobile Weather Apps
    • Local weather stations
    • Digital temperature & humidity sensors

    Recommended Method:
    Weather apps and local weather reports are the cheapest and easiest options for most farmers.

  • Rainfall

    Rainfall indicates how much water the crops naturally receive. Different crops require different rainfall ranges.

    Ways to measure:
    • Weather Apps
    • IMD weather reports
    • Rain gauges
    • Weather APIs

    Recommended Method:
    Local weather apps and IMD reports are reliable, free, and easily accessible.
